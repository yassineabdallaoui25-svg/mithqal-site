About

The mithqal is an old Moroccan unit of weight and currency. We chose the name because our job is to weigh things fairly: a salary, a budget, an expense.

MITHQAL is an independent project, built in Tangier by a financial analyst. The apps run on Android with no account and keep your data on your phone.

Our calculations follow the laws in force (General Tax Code, finance laws, CNSS, Labour Code). Every rule is dated and sourced, and updated with each new finance law.
