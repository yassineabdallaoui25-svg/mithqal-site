What the app does

Dashboard

Your cash and bank balance and the month’s essentials at a glance.

Transactions

Income, expenses, cash ↔ bank transfers, starting balance.

Monthly budgets

4 ready-made templates and alerts at 80% and when exceeded.

Debts

“I owe” and “Owed to me”, partial repayments, overdue items.

Savings goals

Set money aside or take it back, and watch your progress.

Analytics

How your spending moved over 6 months.

Backup

Validated JSON export and import, and full deletion in one step.

Privacy

No account: your transactions, budgets, debts and goals stay on your phone and the app works offline. It is free thanks to Google AdMob ads: when you are online, AdMob processes technical data (advertising ID, approximate location, diagnostics), and a consent form appears where the law requires it.

Languages: French, Darija, English.

Is the app free?

Yes. It is funded by Google AdMob ads. Your financial data stays on your phone and is not sent to anyone.

Export a JSON file, copy it to the new phone and import it.
