2027 finance bill: what every salary gains

Under Morocco’s 2027 finance bill, an employee would gain at most MAD 500 net a month, and nothing at all below 5,721.57 MAD gross. We ran the MITHQAL Salary engine on every salary from MAD 3,000 to 50,000. Here is what came out.

5,721.57 MAD

Below 5,721.57 MAD gross a month, nothing changes. These salaries already pay no income tax in 2026, including the minimum wage (3,422.72 MAD gross).

8,374.78 MAD

From 5,721.57 MAD to 8,374.78 MAD gross, income tax would disappear entirely. At 8,374.78 MAD, the gain reaches 331.27 MAD a month.

+500

The gain is capped at MAD 500 a month (6,000 a year), reached from 11,785.10 MAD gross. Someone on 12,000 and a director on 50,000 would gain exactly the same.

+5.05%

In percentage terms, salaries around 10,080 MAD gross gain most: +5.05% take-home pay.

Why MAD 500 at most?

The bill changes two things. It raises the tax-free band from MAD 40,000 to 60,000 a year and shifts the brackets above it. It also replaces the professional-expense rate (35% or 25% depending on salary) with a single 45% rate, keeping the MAD 35,000 annual cap.

For higher salaries, professional expenses are already at the MAD 35,000 cap under both scales. Only the gap between the scales remains: from the 34% bracket up, 2027 tax is MAD 6,000 a year lower, MAD 500 a month, whatever the salary.

What about dependants?

Each dependant already cuts tax by MAD 600 a year. With one dependant, the gross needs to be at least 6,500.01 MAD to see a gain in 2027.

Where is the bill now?

Finance bill no. 60.26 was adopted at first reading by the House of Representatives on 14 November 2026 and is before the House of Councillors. Until it is published in the Official Bulletin, these amounts are estimates. We will update this page and our calculators the day it is published.

Method

Calculated with the MITHQAL Salary engine (the same as in the app and on this site): CNSS 4.48% capped at MAD 6,000, AMO 2.26%, professional expenses, annual income-tax scale, rounded to the centime. Private-sector employee, no bonus, no complementary insurance, no dependants unless stated. The gain is the annual income-tax difference divided by 12 (CNSS and AMO do not change); net 2027 shown = net 2026 + gain. A month-by-month calculation can differ by one centime.
