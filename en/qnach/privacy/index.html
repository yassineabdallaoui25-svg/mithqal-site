Privacy policy — MITHQAL QNACH

Version 1.0 · Effective since 26 November 2026

Contact: yassine.abdallaoui25@gmail.com

MITHQAL QNACH helps shopkeepers keep their customer credit book: who owes how much, since when and what has been repaid. The app is free and funded by Google AdMob ads.

Data you enter

Customers (name, phone number if you add it), credits, repayments, notes and settings are stored only on your device. There is no account, no cloud and no MITHQAL server: this data is never sent to the developer, to Google or to anyone else.

Your customers’ data

You are recording information about other people. Use it only to manage their credit and respect their privacy.

WhatsApp reminders

When you tap “Reminder”, the app opens WhatsApp with a ready-made message and the exact amount. Nothing is sent automatically: you are the one who sends the message, which is then processed by WhatsApp under its own rules.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

To show ads, measure their performance and fight fraud, AdMob uses the internet and may collect information about the device: advertising ID, IP address, approximate location, device model and interactions with ads. This data is processed by Google under its own rules: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. AdMob never receives your credit book data.

Your consent

Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom), the app shows Google’s consent form before any personalised ad. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings.

What the developer receives

Only the aggregate, anonymous ad revenue statistics provided by AdMob. No account, no MITHQAL server.

Backups

Backups are protected by AES-256 encryption and let you restore your credit book on a new phone. The backup file stays under your control.

Deletion

Uninstalling the app deletes all its data from the device. For advertising data held by Google, see https://policies.google.com/privacy.

Children

The app is not intended for children under 13.

Changes

If the app changes, this page will be updated before the new version is released, with a new date.
