What the app does

Customers and credit

Every customer with their balance. Record a credit in seconds.

Repayments

Partial, full or in advance: the balance updates by itself.

Trustworthy history

Nothing is silently erased: a mistake is fixed with a visible entry, like a real ledger.

WhatsApp reminder

One tap opens the customer’s chat with a polite message and the exact amount. You send it.

Dashboard and reports

What you are owed in total at a glance, and reports on your activity.

Encrypted backup

Backup protected with AES-256 encryption, and restore on a new phone.

Privacy

No account, no cloud, no server: your notebook stays on your phone. Backups are encrypted. The app is free thanks to Google AdMob ads, with a consent form where the law requires it.

FAQ

Is the app free?

Yes. It is funded by Google AdMob ads.

Do my customers get automatic messages?

No. The app prepares the message in WhatsApp; you decide whether to send it.

What if I change phones?

Make an encrypted backup, keep the file somewhere safe, then restore it on the new phone.
