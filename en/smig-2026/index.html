Minimum wage 2026 in Morocco: gross, net and history

Since 1 January 2026, the minimum wage in industry, trade and services is 17.92 MAD an hour. Here’s what that means on a payslip.

17.92 MADan hour since 1 January 2026 (SMIG)
3,422.72 MADgross a month, based on 191 hours
3,192.03 MADnet a month after CNSS and AMO, no income tax
97.44 MADa day in agriculture since 1 April 2026 (SMAG)

At the minimum wage, taxable income stays below the 40,000 MAD exempt band, so there’s no income tax. The employer also pays 721.84 MAD in employer contributions, a total cost of 4,144.56 MAD a month.

FAQ

Is the minimum wage subject to income tax?

No. A minimum-wage salary stays below the exempt band (40,000 MAD of taxable income a year). Only CNSS and AMO are deducted.

Why 191 hours a month?

The legal working time is 2,288 hours a year outside agriculture (44 hours a week). Payslips commonly use 191 hours a month. With 2,288 / 12 = 190.67 hours, the monthly SMIG would be 3,416.75 MAD.

What will the minimum wage be in 2027?

No decree has set a 2027 SMIG so far. The 2026 amount stays in force until a new decree is published.

Sources and date checked

  • Decree no. 2.25.983 (Official Bulletin no. 7469, 29 December 2025): SMIG 17.92 MAD an hour from 1 January 2026, SMAG 97.44 MAD a day from 1 April 2026.
  • Decrees no. 2.19.422, 2.22.606, 2.23.799 and 2.24.1122 for earlier amounts.
  • Labour Code (law 65-99), art. 356 to 361. Checked on 27 November 2026.
