Try the apps before everyone else

MITHQAL Salary, Budget and QNACH are in closed testing on Google Play. To install them, you just need to be on the tester list. It’s free.

Free, no account, works offline. Until the apps launch on Google Play.

On iPhone: tap Safari’s Share button, then “Add to Home Screen”.

How it works

  1. Send your Gmail address

    It’s the address of your Google Play account on Android. One message covers all three apps.

  2. Get the install link

    As soon as your address is on the list, you get the Google Play link by email.

  3. Keep the app for at least 14 days

    Google asks testers to keep the app installed for 14 days before the public release. If something’s off, just reply to the email.

Why a closed test?

Google requires every new app to go through a test with at least 12 testers for 14 days before it can be released to everyone. Every tester brings the public launch closer.

Good to know

  • Android only for now.
  • None of the apps asks for an account: your figures stay on your phone.
  • Your address is only used to add you to the tester list.
