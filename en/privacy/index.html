Website privacy policy

Updated: 27 November 2026

What we do not collect

The website requires no account or sign-up. Amounts entered in the calculators are processed in your browser and never sent to our servers.

Audience measurement

When audience measurement is active, it is cookieless and only counts visits and clicks in aggregate (page views, country, device type, visit source). It cannot identify you.

Hosting

The website is served by a host and content-delivery network that technically processes IP addresses to deliver pages and protect the site from abuse.

Email

If you write to us, we use your address only to reply (and, if you ask, to register you as a tester).

Advertising

This website shows Google AdSense ads. Google and its partners use cookies to serve ads based on your previous visits to this website or other websites. You can turn off personalised advertising in Google Ads Settings (https://adssettings.google.com). Learn more: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. Your consent is requested where the law requires it.

Your rights

Under Moroccan law 09-08, you can request access to, correction or deletion of your data by writing to us.
