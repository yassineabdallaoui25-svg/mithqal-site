What the app does

Gross ↔ net

Detailed CNSS, AMO, professional expenses and income tax, with dependants. 2026 rules plus a clearly labelled preview of the 2027 bill.

Payslip scanner

The photo is read on your phone and then discarded: never sent, never stored. Check your CNSS declaration.

My month

A dashboard that sums up your month and suggests one next action.

My payslips

Keep a numbers-only summary of each month (no photo), up to 24 months, and see what changed.

Work and leaving

Overtime, pay rise, final settlement.

Retirement and financial life

CNSS pension estimate and a 5, 10 or 15-year simulator with your plans.

Savings

Goals, 52-week and Ramadan challenges, daret tracking.

Privacy

No account, no sign-up. Your amounts and calculations stay on your phone. The app is free thanks to Google AdMob ads shown only on the Learn and Saved screens, never next to your result.

Languages: French, Darija, English.

Salary app privacy

FAQ

Is the app free?

Yes. It shows ads on two screens (Learn and Saved), never next to your result.

Is my data sent anywhere?

No. Calculations happen on the phone and there is no account. Details are in the privacy policy.

Are the results official?

They are estimates based on the laws in force, with sources. Your payslip may differ (bonuses, insurance, employer rounding).
