How net pay is calculated

  1. Hourly wage = monthly gross / 191 hours.
  2. 25% premium between 6:00 and 21:00, 50% between 21:00 and 6:00.
  3. On the weekly rest day: 50% by day, 100% at night.
  4. Overtime is salary: CNSS (within its cap), AMO and income tax apply. The net gain uses the 2026 rules.

Frequently asked questions

Does it apply to agriculture?

No. In agriculture the daytime band is 5:00–20:00. This calculator follows non-agricultural activities.

Why is the net gain lower?

Overtime is added to taxable salary: it is taxed at your marginal income-tax rate and subject to AMO.

Rules and verification date

  • Code du travail (loi 65-99), art. 196 à 203 — heures supplémentaires: majoration de 25 % (6 h–21 h) et 50 % (21 h–6 h), portée à 50 % et 100 % le jour du repos hebdomadaire — Verified 24/11/2026. Non-agricultural activities; agriculture uses 5 h–20 h. Hourly wage = monthly / 191 h.
  • Loi de finances n° 60-24 pour 2025 (BO n° 7362, 19/12/2024) — barème art. 73-I, frais professionnels art. 59-I — Bracket table applies to income from 1 Jan 2025; not modified by LF 2026.
  • CNSS — taux de cotisation (prestations sociales 4,48 % salarié plafonné à 6 000 MAD, dont IPE 0,19 %; AMO 2,26 % sans plafond) — IPE (loi 03-14, 1/12/2014) raised the employee share from 4.29 % to 4.48 %.

Rules verified on 24 November 2026.
