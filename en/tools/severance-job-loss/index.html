Severance pay and job-loss allowance (IPE)

How net pay is calculated

  1. Severance: after 6 months of service, except serious misconduct. Each year or part of a year counts.
  2. 96 hours of pay per year for years 1–5, 144 h for years 6–10, 192 h for years 11–15, 240 h beyond.
  3. Hourly wage = average monthly salary over the last 52 weeks / 191 hours.
  4. IPE (CNSS): 70% of the average salary of the last 36 months, capped at the minimum wage, for 6 months.
  5. IPE conditions: involuntary loss, 780 declared days over 36 months including 260 in the last 12, claim within 60 days.

Frequently asked questions

Does resigning give any of this?

No severance and no IPE. Days worked and untaken leave are still owed.

What is the 2026 minimum wage?

MAD 17.92 an hour, i.e. MAD 3,422.72 gross for 191 hours a month (decree 2.25.983). It is also the IPE monthly cap.

Rules and verification date

  • Indemnité pour perte d’emploi (loi 03-14, art. 46 bis du dahir 1-72-184) — 780 jours sur 36 mois dont 260 sur les 12 derniers; 70 % du salaire moyen des 36 derniers mois, plafonné au SMIG; 6 mois; demande sous 60 jours — Reform announced by the social-protection framework law, not enacted as of 2026-11-20.
  • Décret n° 2.25.983 (BO n° 7469, 29/12/2025) — SMIG non agricole 17,92 MAD/heure au 1/1/2026; 191 heures par mois — 17.92 × 191 = 3,422.72 MAD gross per month.
  • Code du travail (loi 65-99), art. 52 à 55 — indemnité de licenciement après 6 mois: 96 h/an (1–5 ans), 144 h (6–10), 192 h (11–15), 240 h (au-delà), par année ou fraction d’année — Hourly wage = average monthly salary of the last 52 weeks / 191 hours.

Rules verified on 20 November 2026.
