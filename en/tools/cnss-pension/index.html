How net pay is calculated

  1. Legal age: 60.
  2. Reference salary: average of the last 96 declared months, capped at MAD 6,000 a month.
  3. At least 1,320 days: rate = 50% × days / 3,240 (below 3,240 days).
  4. 3,240 days: 50%. Then +1 point per full 216 days, up to 70% (7,560 days).
  5. Minimum MAD 600 to 1,000 depending on days. Maximum pension: MAD 4,200 a month.

Frequently asked questions

Why is my pension capped?

CNSS contributions and pensions are based on a salary capped at MAD 6,000 a month. At the 70% maximum rate, the CNSS pension cannot exceed MAD 4,200 a month.

What if I have fewer than 1,320 days?

There is no pension: you can request a refund of your employee share of contributions.

Is the pension reform included?

No. The calculator applies only the rules in force, including decree 2.25.265. A reform that is announced but not adopted is not used.

Rules and verification date

  • CNSS — Pension de vieillesse (régime général): 50 % à 3 240 jours, +1 point par 216 jours, max. 70 %; salaire de référence = moyenne des 96 derniers mois déclarés, plafonné à 6 000 MAD; minimum 1 000 MAD — Age 60 (55 for underground miners with 5 years). Checked 2026-11-20.
  • Décret n° 2.25.265 (loi 02.24) — pension dès 1 320 jours: Pn = Tx × Sr, Tx = 50 % × jours / 3 240 (2 décimales); minimums 600 à 1 000 MAD — In force 1 May 2025, retroactive to retirements from 1 Jan 2023.
