How to read your payslip

Taxable gross salary
Your base salary plus taxable bonuses (seniority, performance, overtime). It’s the base for AMO and income tax.
CNSS
4.48% of gross pay, calculated on 6,000 MAD at most. So the deduction never exceeds 268.80 MAD.
AMO
2.26% of the full gross, with no cap.
Professional expenses
A 35% allowance (25% above 6,500 MAD gross a month) that reduces the income tax base. It doesn’t always appear on the payslip.
Income tax
Calculated on net taxable salary using the progressive brackets, minus 50 MAD a month per dependant (up to 6).
Net pay
Gross minus deductions, plus tax-free allowances, minus advances and other deductions.

The most common mistakes

  • Dependants not declared to the employer: income tax is too high.
  • CNSS calculated on the whole salary instead of the 6,000 MAD cap.
  • Meal or transport allowance above the cap treated as tax-free.
  • An old income tax scale still in use after a finance law.

FAQ

Is my data sent anywhere?

No. The calculation runs in your browser. Nothing is saved or sent.

Does the check apply to civil servants?

No. It follows the rules for private-sector employees registered with the CNSS.

What if a line is wrong?

Print or save the report as a PDF and show it to your payroll team. The calculation is an estimate for information, not legal advice.
