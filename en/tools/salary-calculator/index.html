How net pay is calculated

  1. CNSS: 4.48% of gross, capped at MAD 6,000 a month (MAD 268.80 at most).
  2. AMO: 2.26% of gross, no cap.
  3. Professional expenses: a percentage of annual gross, capped (see table).
  4. Net taxable income = gross − professional expenses − CNSS − AMO (annual).
  5. Income tax: annual progressive brackets, minus the dependant reduction, divided by 12.
  6. Net = gross − CNSS − AMO − income tax.

Frequently asked questions

What is the employee CNSS rate?

4.48% of gross salary capped at MAD 6,000 a month (including 0.19% for the job-loss allowance), plus 2.26% AMO on the whole gross.

What changes in 2027?

The 2027 finance bill raises the tax-free band from MAD 40,000 to 60,000 a year and sets professional expenses at 45% (cap MAD 35,000). It passed its first reading in the House of Representatives on 14 November 2026 and is not yet enacted.

Why does my net drop when gross passes MAD 6,500?

In 2026 the professional-expense rate falls from 35% to 25% above MAD 78,000 of annual gross. Just above that threshold, net pay can fall by about MAD 65 a month.

Does it work for civil servants?

No. This calculator covers private-sector employees registered with CNSS.
