Why you don’t keep the whole raise

  1. CNSS takes 4.48% while your gross stays under 6,000 MAD. Above that, it stops growing.
  2. AMO takes 2.26% of every dirham, with no cap.
  3. Income tax applies at your marginal rate: each extra dirham is taxed at the highest bracket you reach (up to 37%).
  4. At 6,500 MAD gross (78,000 MAD a year), the professional expenses allowance drops from 35% to 25%. It’s the only point where a higher gross can lower your net.

FAQ

Why does my net drop if my gross goes from 6,500 to 6,510 MAD?

At 6,500 MAD gross, your net is 6,036.70 MAD. At 6,510 MAD it falls to 5,980.75 MAD, because the 35% allowance no longer applies. You need to reach about 6,600 MAD (6,062.17 MAD) to come out ahead.

How much of a raise do I usually keep?

Around 93% on a low salary, about 83% at 8,000 MAD, and just over 60% above 15,000 MAD gross.

How do I negotiate in net terms?

Decide the net you want, then convert it to gross with the calculator’s “the net I want” mode. The gross is what goes in the contract.
